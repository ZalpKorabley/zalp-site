ZALP is a community-driven mod installer for **Мир кораблей**, dedicated to providing players with a seamless mod management and installation experience.

## Features

- **Mod Management**：Easily browse, install, and update mods
- **Community-Driven**：Maintained and updated by the player community
- **Multi-Language Support**：Interface supports multiple languages for players worldwide
